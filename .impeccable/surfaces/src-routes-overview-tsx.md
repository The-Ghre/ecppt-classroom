## Direction contract

THESIS: A navy operations console for teaching a penetration test — the interface is a precise instrument, not a slide theme. It refuses the two category defaults it sits between: the generic dark dashboard (card grid of equal tiles) and the decorative hacker terminal (Matrix rain, phosphor green, fake CLI). Every surface reads as calibrated measurement: the course's own thesis, "measure, don't guess," rendered as the chrome.

OWN-WORLD: CODED navy only — ground #00081c/#00112F, console panels #0e2140/#12294f/#16294a, hairline graticule rgba(255,255,255,.06–.12) ruling diagrams and panel frames. IBM Plex Sans display (tight negative tracking) over IBM Plex Mono readouts/coordinates/labels (uppercase, 2.5–4px tracking). Semantic ink NEVER alone — always icon + label + shape: blue #6f9ce8 evidence/method/defender, red #f84b4b attacker/exploit/finding, green #5ad0a0 validated, gold #e0b45c caution/assumption, cyan #79e2ec + violet #c8adff secondary channels. Recognizable with all content removed by: the ruled measuring grid, the mono coordinate/readout strips, square instrument corners (radius 6–14px), and the attack-stage spine.

STORY: A student understands that a pentest is a disciplined chain of evidence→validation→impact; believes CODED's course will make them methodical, not just tool-happy; and acts by entering a session (present) or reading it deeply (study), resuming where they left off.

FIRST VIEWPORT (overview): Masthead left — "CODED · eCPPT" wordmark, mono coordinate line (dates 15 Nov–16 Dec 2026, 15 sessions). Below, the five-week roadmap as a horizontal console: five week-columns on the ruled grid, each holding its session modules (theory/practical marked by shape + label, not color), the attack-chain spine (recon→access→escalate→pivot→AD→impact) running across as the organizing axis. Primary action: a prominent "Enter / Resume" control on the active session. Right: a compact legend + progress readout.

FORM: Blueprint Console — "Impeccable's Pick," the user's chosen grounded direction, played straight at full fidelity (no irony, no smuggled quirk). Raised by two donated disciplines: exploded numbered step-grammar (attack-chain/escalation diagrams: prior stages ghosted, current bright with a leader arrow seating into the grid) and the graticule (everything measured against a ruled grid). Seed key 58f0c4e3, assigned index 4 declined in favor of the pick.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
